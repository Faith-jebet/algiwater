About Us

Algi Water and Energy Engineering project approach entails:

  • • Engineering: our engineering team is well versed with the water and energy needs and issues across Kenya. We have designed and executed numerous projects across the country.Our process involves need analysis, scoping, system design,costing, cost-benefit analysis, installation and construction aswell as operation and maintenance.
  • • Procurement: the execution of a good project ismainlydependent on great design and quality products. We select the most optimal equipment fromthe top manufacturers across the world. At all times, our primary focus is on ensuring that we are offering high quality products, besides delivering our services effectively and efficiently to all our customers.
  • • Construction: Our technical team includes highly experienced engineers and technicians.They employ cutting-edge technology while executing our projects. In addition, they run operation and maintenance programmes for the said projects.
    • Our model enables us to maintain responsibility for all the process stages, starting from design,procurement, construction to commissioning and handing over the project to the end-user.This allows our clients to have a stress-free experience.
