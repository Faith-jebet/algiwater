Reverse Osmosis Systems

Reverse Osmosis (RO) technology goes beyond the abilities of typical water filtration by using a semi-permeable membrane to remove dissolved salts and impurities from water. Reverse osmosis water filtration provides a low-maintenance, chemical-free option to help achieve clean, great-tasting water every day.

What We Offer:

  • Low-maintenance, chemical-free filtration
  • Removal of dissolved salts and impurities

Sand Filters

Sand filtration is used for the removal of suspended matter, as well as floating and sinkable particles. The wastewater flows vertically through a fine bed of sand and gravel. Surface filtration collects particles above the filter bed, while depth filtration removes smaller particles bound to sand particles by adsorption.

What We Offer:

  • Removal of suspended and floating particles
  • Surface and depth filtration
  • Vertical sand and gravel bed systems

Ultra-Filtration

Ultrafiltration (UF) is a process in which hydrostatic pressure forces a liquid against a semi-permeable membrane. Suspended solids and solutes of high molecular weight are retained, while water and low molecular weight solutes pass through the membrane.

What We Offer:

  • Semi-permeable membrane filtration
  • Retention of suspended solids
  • High molecular weight solute removal

Water Softeners

The presence of calcium and magnesium carbonates in water makes the water hard, causing scaling and damage to water systems such as boilers, electric household appliances, valves and taps. Our wide range of water softeners gets rid of calcium and magnesium, extending the life of your appliances and fittings.

What We Offer:

  • Calcium and magnesium removal
  • Prevention of scaling and appliance damage
  • Improved water for laundry and household use

Sterilization
